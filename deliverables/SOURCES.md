# Research sources & fact check

Every figure shown on screen comes from Google's official press release. I read it directly and captured the screenshots on 20 Nov 2026.

## Primary source (used on screen)

**Google Cloud Press Corner, "Google Announces Digital Infrastructure Expansion, Local Jobs, Water Replenishment, and Clean Energy Initiatives with US$15 Billion Malaysia Investment"** (4 November 2026)
https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-11-04-Google-Announces-Digital-Infrastructure-Expansion-Local-Jobs-Water-Replenishment-and-Clean-Energy-Initiatives-with-US-15-Billion-Malaysia-Investment

| On-screen claim | Speaker line it supports | Exact wording in source |
|---|---|---|
| US$15 bilion, 2027–2028 | "Google nak labur 15 bilion USD di Malaysia" | "an investment of US$15 billion over the next two years (2027 to 2028)" |
| Elmina Business Park (operational); new campuses planned in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan | "data center ini bukan sekadar bangunan…" | "Google's first data center campus in Elmina Business Park, Selangor—which is now operational—and new, planned campuses in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan" |
| 37,000+ local jobs/yr during construction; ≈6,000 in construction; US$1.1B/yr to GDP | "lebih 37 ribu pekerjaan setahun semasa pembinaan" | "contribute an annual average of US$1.1 billion to Malaysia's GDP and support more than 37,000 local jobs per year … Approximately 6,000 of these jobs are estimated to be in construction." |
| 9,000+ local jobs/yr once operational; wages averaging 45% above the national median | "Bila beroperasi nanti lebih 9 ribu" | "Once operational, the facilities are projected to sustain more than 9,000 local jobs per year, with wages averaging 45% above the national median." |
| Ecosystem: on-site specialists, contractors with local suppliers, shops, restaurants and amenities | "Ini kerja yang diwujudkan oleh ekosistem Google, bukan semuanya dengan Google" / "Jurutera, juruteknik, kontraktor, orang logistik" | "…data center technicians, mechanical engineers, electrical engineers, logistics operations managers, and physical security managers—as well as contractor roles with local suppliers and workers at the shops, restaurants, and amenities…" |
| 80+ Malaysian entities in the domestic supply chain | "berapa banyak syarikat tempatan dapat peluang" | "Google is working with a domestic supply chain of more than 80 Malaysian entities … spanning construction and civil engineering; network infrastructure; power and cooling system components; and services like logistics…" |
| Data Center Institutes at polytechnics in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan, TVET, from 2027 | "berapa ramai rakyat Malaysia dapat kerja yang bagus" | "Google plans to establish DCIs in selected polytechnics in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan. Starting in 2027, this initiative aims to train and place local … (TVET) jobseekers for data center deployment and operations roles." |

The on-screen notes say that the job, GDP and wage figures are **Google's own estimates and projections**.

## Deliberately left out
- Clean energy (1 GW), water replenishment, the commitment to pay for 100% of power, and the 800,000-person upskilling target. All of these are verified in the release, but the speaker doesn't discuss them, so they weren't forced into the video.
